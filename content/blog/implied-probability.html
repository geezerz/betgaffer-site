Why a bookmaker's percentages add up to more than 100%

Here's a match result market, with example odds again:

These are example odds, not real prices.

ResultExample oddsImplied probability
Home2.0050.0%
Draw3.4029.4%
Away4.0025.0%
Total104.4%

Real chances can't add up to more than 100%. The extra 4.4% is the bookmaker's margin, built into the prices. So each outcome's implied probability is a little higher than it would be without the margin.
