There's no such thing as half a goal. The ".5" just means there's no draw-like middle: two goals or fewer loses, three or more wins.

How it settles

Total goalsOver 2.5Under 2.5
0, 1 or 2LostWon
3 or moreWonLost

Most bookmakers settle it on the score at the end of regular time, including added time, so extra time and penalties don't count. Rules differ by bookmaker, so check yours.

Examples

These are example scores, not real matches.

  • 2–1: three goals, so Over 2.5 wins.
  • 1–1: two goals, so Over 2.5 loses.
  • 3–0: three goals, so Over 2.5 wins. It doesn't matter which team scores them.

The higher the line, the more goals you need, and the less often it happens.

Some bookmakers let you switch on an early payout for Over bets. See {{article:early-goals:EarlyGoals, explained}}.

Takeaway: Over 2.5 is three goals or more, from either side. Count the goals, not the winner.
