Most bookmakers settle it on the score at the end of regular time, including added time, so extra time and penalties don't count. Rules differ by bookmaker, so check yours.

These are example odds, not real prices.

Win or Draw (double chance "1X")WonWonLost1.25

The more results that count in your favour, the shorter the odds. None of the three is "better". Each one swaps price for cover.

When people use it

Draw No Bet is used when you think a team will win but a draw is a real chance, such as two evenly matched sides.

Takeaway: Draw No Bet protects you from the draw, not from a loss.
