It can settle your Over bet early, before the match is over.

How many goals, by which minute

Your betSettled as won early if (SportyBet's wording)…
Over 1.51 goal by 10 min
Over 2.52 goals by 30 min
Over 3.53 goals by 50 min

SportyBet doesn't say how goals right on the minute, or in stoppage time, count, so check your slip.

What if the early goals don't come?

Then nothing is paid early, and your bet is treated as a normal Over/Under bet, settled on the total goals.

Where you can use it

EarlyGoals is only offered on Over/Under markets in some leagues.

Rules can change, so check SportyBet's own rules.

From SportyBet's betslip help, checked {{checked_long}}:

  • "If a number of goals is scored by a specified minute, your Over selection is immediately settled as winning"
  • "Over 1.5 - 1 goal by 10 min • Over 2.5 - 2 goal by 30 min • Over 3.5 - 3 goal by 50 min"
  • "If the EarlyGoals condition is not met, your selection is treated as a normal Over/Under bet."
  • "EarlyGoals is only available on O/U markets in specific leagues."

Easy to get wrong

  • It's only for Over bets. It doesn't apply to Under.
  • Not every league has it. SportyBet offers it on Over/Under markets in some leagues only.
  • Missing the early goals isn't a loss. Your bet is then settled as a normal Over/Under bet.
