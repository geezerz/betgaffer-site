It's a win bet that gives your stake back if the match is drawn.

If your team wins, your bet wins. If the match is a draw, you get your stake back. If your team loses, you lose.

Most bookmakers settle it on the score at the end of regular time, including added time, so extra time and penalties don't count. Rules differ by bookmaker, so check yours.

A worked example

You back the home team at Asian Handicap 0 with ₦1,000, at odds of 1.50.

These are example odds, not real prices.

How it differs from a straight Win

On a straight Win (the "1" in 1X2), a draw loses. Handicap 0 turns that draw into a refund. That refund has a price: the odds are shorter than for a straight Win on the same team.

Is it the same as Draw No Bet?

It settles the same way. Some bookmakers list both, and the odds can differ a little. Rules differ by bookmaker, so check yours.

Easy to get wrong

  • A draw isn't a loss here. Your stake comes back.
  • It isn't a straight win bet. On a straight Win, a draw loses. On Handicap 0, it's a refund.
  • Other handicap lines work differently. This guide covers 0 only. Lines like +0.5 or quarter lines settle by their own rules.

Rules differ by bookmaker, so check yours.

Takeaway: Handicap 0 is a win bet that refunds the draw, and you pay for that with a shorter price.
