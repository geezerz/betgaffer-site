It covers two of the three results, so only one result can beat it.

What each one covers

BetWins ifLoses if
1XHome wins or drawAway wins
X2Away wins or drawHome wins
12Either team winsDraw

Most bookmakers settle it on the score at the end of regular time, including added time, so extra time and penalties don't count. Rules differ by bookmaker, so check yours.

Rules can change, so check SportyBet's own rules.

From SportyBet's football rules, checked {{checked_long}}:

  • "Predict the match result combining two of the three possible outcomes (1X, X2 or 12)."
  • "Regular 90 Minutes: Markets are based on the result at the end of a scheduled 90 minutes play unless otherwise stated."

Easy to get wrong

  • 12 isn't a draw bet. It wins if either team wins, and loses on a draw.
  • More cover, shorter odds. 1X covers the home win and the draw, so its odds are shorter than a straight home win on the same match.
  • Under SportyBet's rules, extra time doesn't count: a cup match level after 90 minutes is a draw for Double Chance too.
