How it settles

Total goalsOver 1.5Under 1.5
0 or 1LostWon
2 or moreWonLost

Most bookmakers settle it on the score at the end of regular time, including added time, so extra time and penalties don't count. Rules differ by bookmaker, so check yours.

Examples

These are example scores, not real matches.

  • 1–1: two goals, so Over 1.5 wins.
  • 1–0: one goal, so Over 1.5 loses.
  • 2–0: two goals, so Over 1.5 wins. It doesn't matter which team scores them.

The higher the line, the more goals you need, and the less often it happens. See {{article:over-2-5-goals:Over 2.5 goals, explained}}.

Takeaway: Over 1.5 is two goals or more, from either side.
