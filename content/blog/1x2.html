It's also the bet the other result bets are built on, so learn this one and the rest get easier.

How it settles

These are example scores, not real matches. The home team is listed first.

Final score1X2
2–1WonLostLost
1–1LostWonLost
0–2LostLostWon

SportyBet settles it on the result at the end of the scheduled 90 minutes. Extra time and penalty shootouts don't count. If a match is interrupted or postponed and isn't continued within 48 hours of the original kick-off, bets on it are void.

Easy to get wrong

  • "1" is the home team, not the better team. Check which side is listed first.
  • Under SportyBet's rules, extra time doesn't count: a cup match level after 90 minutes is an X, even if one team goes through later.
  • A draw beats both teams. Double Chance and Draw No Bet cover the draw: see them below.

Rules can change, so check SportyBet's own rules.

From SportyBet's football rules, checked {{checked_long}}:

  • "Which team will win the match. Overtime not included."
  • "Regular 90 Minutes: Markets are based on the result at the end of a scheduled 90 minutes play unless otherwise stated."
  • "If a match is interrupted or postponed and is not continued within 48h after initial kick-off date betting will be void."
  • "...but does not include extra-time, time allocated for a penalty shootout or golden goal."

Takeaway: 1X2 is the result at the end of the scheduled 90 minutes: home, draw or away.
