Don't take our word for it. Check it.

Picks that quietly change after kick-off, and winning screenshots with the losses left out, can make people stop trusting predictions. BetGaffer is built the other way round: picks are set to freeze before kick-off, our record is public, misses included, and you can check it yourself.

What is a frozen pick?

A frozen pick is final. Once it freezes, it can't be changed or swapped.

That's the pick that stands, whatever happens in the match.

When does a pick freeze?

Each pick is set to freeze no later than 30 minutes before kick-off.

From that moment until the final whistle, the pick doesn't move. Once a pick has frozen, our system doesn't swap it, whatever happens in the match.

How do I know a pick is frozen?

On the page, a frozen pick carries a padlock.

Tap it to see "Locked at" and the time, in Lagos time, with the words "This pick locked before kick-off and won't change now."

What's a receipt code?

Every pick shows its price when it was listed, and the receipt code for the day's card is on the page.

Think of the receipt code as a seal on the day's card: change a single pick and the code no longer fits.

Can I check a ticket or card someone sends me?

Yes. Anyone can, member or not, on our Check an ID page.

Every ticket and card shared from BetGaffer carries an ID. Type it in and you'll see what we saved for it, then compare that with the image you were sent. If we have no record of the ID, the page says so.

You can check a card or ticket by its ID for 30 days after it was shared or built.

Can I see the misses too?

Yes. Our picks are graded in public, misses included, month by month.

Every accuracy figure tells you how many picks it covers and which dates.
