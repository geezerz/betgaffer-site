2UP works the same way, but your team has to go two goals ahead.

Home team never leads, and the match ends 1–1Not paid early
Home team never leads, and the match ends 0–2Not paid early

On 2UP, swap "one goal ahead" for "two goals ahead". A 2–0 or 3–1 lead settles it as won.

What if your team leads and then loses?

Once a 1UP or 2UP bet is settled, the final score doesn't change it. A team that leads 1–0 and loses 1–2 still pays on 1UP.

What about the draw?

Backing the draw in this market works like a normal 1X2 draw bet. It's settled on the score at full time.

SportyBet's rules don't say how a 1UP or 2UP bet settles when it isn't paid early, so check your slip or SportyBet's help before you bet.

  • 1UP and 2UP are options you switch on for a 1X2 pick, and only in some leagues. Both teams can end up winning: in 2UP, a match that turns from 2–0 to 2–4 pays both sides.

Never Down, the opposite idea

Never Down pays only if your team wins without ever falling behind. If they go a goal down at any point, the bet loses, even if they come back to win.
